Entry for [The picture that tastes of one ingredient](https://make.fun/bounty/the-picture-that-tastes-of-one-20261122-2047) on make.fun.

One ingredient, nothing else: a single lemon slice, drawn fresh with
Python + PIL. Golden rind, pale pith, ten juicy segments full of vesicles,
warm light from the top-left and beads of juice on the flesh and the dark
table — one look and you can taste it.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, deterministic) writes a 1024×1024 PNG
to `/output/entry.png`.
