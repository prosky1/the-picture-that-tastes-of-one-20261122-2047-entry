# The Picture That Tastes of One Ingredient — One Glossy Red Chilli

Entry for [the-picture-that-tastes-of-one-20261122-2047](https://make.fun/bounty/the-picture-that-tastes-of-one-20261122-2047) on make.fun.

One ingredient, nothing else: a single glossy red chilli resting on a dark
stone board. The pod curves in a low S, lit by a warm glow against a
charcoal background — bright scarlet up top fading to deep red, a glossy
highlight along its upper edge, a warm rim of light underneath, a green
stem with a star-shaped calyx, a few water droplets on the skin, and three
pale seeds scattered on the board. You can almost taste the heat.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Requires Python 3.13 and Pillow. It writes a single 1024×1024 PNG
(`entry.png`, under 1 MiB) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output` by
default). Deterministic — no network, no randomness beyond a fixed seed.
